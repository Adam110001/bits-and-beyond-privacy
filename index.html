Privacy & support

Bits & Beyond is a local-first learning app. It has no developer-operated account, advertising, analytics, or tracking service.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Data stored on the device

The app stores lesson and reading progress, scores and answers, review schedules, streaks, lab and mission state, bookmarks, saved facts, preferences, interview attempts, and text the learner writes in reflections or debriefs. On iPad, PencilKit Sketchbook drawings and their local Notes entries are also stored on the device. This data remains until the learner resets it or removes the app, subject to device-backup behavior controlled by the operating system.

The app uses this data on the device to recommend what to study next. It is not used for advertising, profiling outside the app, or sale.

Written-answer analysis

On eligible Apple devices, written explanations and interview answers may be evaluated by Apple's Foundation Models framework running on the device. The app does not send those answers to a developer server or an external AI API. If the on-device model is unavailable, the app uses a local rubric or asks the learner to compare the answer manually. Automated feedback may be inaccurate and is a study aid, not an expert assessment.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If the learner turns it on, structured learning data - including written reflections and answers - is copied to the learner's personal iCloud key-value store so it can appear on their Apple devices. PencilKit drawing files and their Sketchbook Notes entries remain on the originating device and are not included in iCloud key-value sync. Apple processes synchronized data under the learner's iCloud account and terms. Bits & Beyond does not operate a server or receive a separate copy.

Turning sync off stops future syncing but does not erase a copy already stored in iCloud. The learner can use Delete iCloud copy in the app's Data & preferences screen to turn sync off and remove that copy.

Optional Apple Watch companion

If the optional Apple Watch companion is installed, the iPhone sends Daily Byte, one due review, streak, and due-count information through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. The Watch caches that snapshot locally for offline use and queues answers for the iPhone. Queued work is not reported as synchronized until the iPhone accepts it. This does not require iCloud sync or send a copy to a developer server.

Optional purchases

Apple processes optional Bits & Beyond Pro purchases through the App Store. StoreKit is the ownership authority. The app keeps a short-lived local cache of verified product, ownership, verification, and expiration details so recently verified access can continue during a temporary connection failure. Purchase ownership is not included in learning backups or optional iCloud learning sync, and resetting learning data does not cancel or erase an App Store purchase.

Notifications, clipboard & exports

Review reminders are optional and are scheduled by the operating system. The app asks for notification permission only after the learner enables reminders.

The app writes to the clipboard only after the learner chooses Copy backup, Copy code, or Copy source link. A manual backup contains learning data and may include learner-written text. It does not contain PencilKit canvas files or their Sketchbook Notes entries; learners can export sketches from Notes before resetting or moving devices. The learner controls where that backup is pasted and stored.

A Notebook export is created on the device only after the learner requests it. It contains learner-written reflections, debriefs, and interview answers, plus saved key facts only when the learner chooses to include them. PDF and Markdown exports can also contain the learner's saved sketch images. The file is passed to the operating system's share sheet, and the learner chooses whether and where to save or share it. Any selected destination processes the file under that destination's own terms.

Retention & learner controls

The Data & preferences screen lets the learner:

  • turn review notifications and iCloud sync off;
  • delete the iCloud copy;
  • copy or import a manual backup;
  • export Notebook writing and sketches or delete Notebook writing; and
  • reset learning data.

If iCloud sync is on, a learning-data reset is synced to the learner's other Apple devices. The reset also removes PencilKit Sketchbook files from the local device. Copied backups can restore the structured data they contain, but not drawings; Notebook exports are reference files and cannot be imported back into Sketchbook. A reset does not remove manual backups, Notebook exports, or device backups; the learner must delete those where they are stored.

Learning claims & references

Labels such as “Completed”, “Strong”, “Solid”, and “Ready” describe activity and evidence within the app. They are not a qualification, certification, promise of interview performance, or independent expert assessment. Source links are provided for follow-up and do not imply publisher endorsement or that every lesson statement has been independently fact-checked.

Support & privacy questions

For app issues, general feedback, feature requests, or privacy questions, email bitsandbeyondsupport@gmail.com. Never include passwords, backup contents, learning answers, or other sensitive personal information in a support request.

Bits & Beyond has no developer account or server-side copy of learner data, so the developer cannot view or delete that data on a learner's behalf. The controls described above manage the device and iCloud copies.
